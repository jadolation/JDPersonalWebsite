Superheroes & Comics

DC & Marvel Universes

Superheroes represent the best of humanity—courage, hope, and the determination to do the right thing even when it's hard. I've been a fan of both DC and Marvel for years, but Superman holds a special place in my heart.

Why Superman? He's the ultimate symbol of hope. Despite having godlike powers, he chooses to be human, to connect with people, and to inspire others to be better. In a world that sometimes feels dark, Superman reminds us that kindness and integrity matter. Plus, that iconic cape and the red "S" on his chest? Timeless.

Beyond Superman, I appreciate the depth of characters like Batman, Spider-Man, Iron Man, and Wonder Woman. Each brings something unique—whether it's Batman's detective skills, Spider-Man's relatability, or Iron Man's genius innovation.

🦸 Superman #1 🦇 Batman 🕷️ Spider-Man ⚡ The Flash 🛡️ Captain America 💚 Hulk ⚔️ Wonder Woman 🤖 Iron Man

Anime & Manga

A Love for Storytelling

Anime isn't just entertainment—it's art, philosophy, and emotion all wrapped into beautifully crafted stories. I love how anime explores themes like perseverance, friendship, ambition, and what it means to chase your dreams.

Tengen Toppa Gurren Lagann is my all-time favorite. It's about breaking through limits, believing in yourself, and piercing the heavens with your determination. The message of "believing in the me that believes in you" resonates deeply—it's a reminder to never give up, even when the odds seem impossible. Plus, the animation and epic scale? Unmatched.

One Piece is another masterpiece. Luffy's unwavering dream to become Pirate King, the bonds of the Straw Hat crew, and the world-building that spans decades—it's storytelling at its finest. Every arc teaches something meaningful about friendship, loyalty, and chasing your dreams no matter how long it takes.

Beyond these, I enjoy a wide range of genres—action, slice-of-life, psychological thrillers, and more. From classics like Cowboy Bebop to modern hits like Demon Slayer and Attack on Titan, each series offers something unique.

🌌 Gurren Lagann 🏴‍☠️ One Piece ⚔️ Demon Slayer 🎯 Hunter x Hunter 🥋 Dragon Ball 🎸 Cowboy Bebop 🔥 My Hero Academia 🗡️ Attack on Titan

Movies & TV Shows

Selective but Passionate

I love movies—especially those that make you think, feel, or see the world differently. From superhero blockbusters to indie dramas, sci-fi epics to thought-provoking thrillers, I appreciate films that tell compelling stories.

When it comes to TV shows, I'm very selective. I don't binge-watch everything that's trending. A show needs to truly grab my attention and deliver consistently great storytelling. So far, I've only finished two series:

1. FRIENDS – The ultimate comfort show. No matter how many times I rewatch it, it never gets old. The humor, the friendships, the nostalgia—it's perfect.

2. Cobra Kai – A brilliant continuation of the Karate Kid saga. It balances action, humor, and heart while exploring themes of redemption, rivalry, and growth. Plus, the fight choreography is incredible.

If a show or movie can make me laugh, cry, or think deeply about life, it's worth my time. Otherwise, I'd rather spend that time coding, working out, or watching anime.

📺 FRIENDS 🥋 Cobra Kai 🎬 Marvel Cinematic Universe 🎥 DC Films 🎞️ Sci-Fi 🍿 Thrillers

Pets & Animals

Dog Lover (Selective with Cats)

Dogs are the best. Their loyalty, energy, and unconditional love make them incredible companions. There's something special about coming home to a wagging tail and pure excitement—no matter how tough the day was.

I especially love dogs that are playful, smart, and full of personality. Whether it's going for walks, playing fetch, or just chilling together, dogs remind us to live in the moment and appreciate the simple joys in life.

Cats? I can appreciate them—especially if they have a unique personality or are particularly affectionate. But I'm selective. Dogs will always have my heart. 🐾
