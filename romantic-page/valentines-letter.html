I'm Sorry, My Love

I need to say something important...

I'm truly sorry for these past bad days. School, stress, reviews - I know they're not excuses, but I want you to understand what's been happening. These challenges have made me less patient, less understanding, and less present than you deserve. I've been so caught up in my own world that I forgot to be there for you the way you've always been there for me.

You've always been my rock, my safe place, my constant source of support. Through every exam, every stressful moment, every doubt I've had - you were there. You listened when I needed to vent, you encouraged me when I wanted to give up, you believed in me even when I didn't believe in myself. And how have I repaid that? By being distant, by being irritable, by not giving you the attention and care you deserve.

I see now how my actions have affected you, and it breaks my heart. You deserve someone who shows up, not just physically but emotionally too. You deserve someone who makes time for you, who listens to you, who cherishes every moment with you. I want to be that person for you - I know I can be, because I have been before, and I'm committed to being that person again.

I'm here now. Really, truly here. Present and committed to us, to you, to everything we're building together. I'm working on myself, on managing my stress better, on being more mindful of how I show up in our relationship. If you still need time and space, if you're not ready to talk right now, I completely understand, and I will wait.

I'll wait as long as you need because you're worth it. You're worth every moment, every effort, every change I need to make. When you're ready, I'll be here with open arms and an open heart, ready to show you every day how much you mean to me. I promise to do better, to be better, because you deserve nothing less than the best version of me.

With deepest regret and endless love,

Jan Dale

I Love You

So Much, Much, Much

Valentine's Day isn't just February 14th for me. It's every single day I get to spend with you. It's not about the roses, the chocolates, or the fancy dinners - though I want to give you all of those too. It's about the everyday moments that make my life beautiful because you're in it.

It's every laugh we share when something silly happens, every deep conversation we have at 2 AM when neither of us can sleep, every time you look at me with those beautiful eyes and I forget what I was saying. It's the way you understand me without words, the way you make me feel safe, the way you believe in our future together.

It's in the quiet moments when we're just together, content in each other's presence, not needing to fill the silence because being near you is enough. It's in the exciting adventures we embark on, the new places we explore, the memories we create that I know I'll treasure forever. Every moment with you becomes a memory I want to hold onto.

You are very dear to me

Every day with you is Valentine's Day because every day you choose to be with me is a gift. Every day you share your thoughts, your dreams, your fears with me is a privilege. Every day you love me despite my flaws is a celebration. You see me - really see me - the good and the bad, and you choose to stay. That's the greatest gift anyone has ever given me.

I love how you make me want to be better. I love how you challenge me to grow while accepting me exactly as I am. I love your strength, your kindness, your intelligence, your humor. I love the way you care about the people in your life. I love your dreams and how passionate you are about the things that matter to you. I love everything about you, even the little things you think are flaws.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for being mine. Thank you for every moment we've shared and every moment we will share. Thank you for your patience, your love, your trust. Thank you for choosing me, every single day. Thank you for making my life infinitely better just by being in it. I promise to spend every day showing you how much you mean to me, how much I appreciate you, how deeply and completely I love you.

Forever and Always,

My Love is Yours

All my love, today and every day,

Jan Dale D. Zarate
