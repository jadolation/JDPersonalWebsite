SRV: Serbisyo, Rito, Valid

Your Local Service Connection in Baguio

Overview

SRV is a web application being developed by "SRV Digital Solutions Co.", designed to act as a centralized digital marketplace connecting Clients seeking various services with independent, local freelance Service Providers, initially focusing on Baguio City and its neighboring areas in the Philippines.

Key Features

Service Discovery

Browse categories, search services, or view providers on a map

Provider Profiles

Detailed profiles with skills, pricing, and verified credentials

Booking System

Request services, set schedules, and communicate via in-app chat

Ratings & Reviews

Build trust through verified user feedback and ratings

Geolocation

Find service providers operating near your location

Payment Options

Cash and GCash payments with in-app confirmation

Available Service Categories

Home Repairs Automobile Repairs Gadget & Appliance Technicians Cleaning Services Beauty Services Delivery and Errands Tutor

Blockchain-Powered Trust System

Innovative vouching and staking mechanism for enhanced trust:

Vouching Mechanism

Users vouch for service providers by staking coins, predicting they'll earn good ratings (3-5 stars)

Smart Contracts

Stakes are locked until targets are met; successful vouches return stakes plus discounts

Reputation Building

Visible vouches signal trustworthiness; only verified clients can rate

Coin Acquisition

Users earn vouching coins (2% of payments) when paying with stablecoins

Market Opportunity

Growing Gig Economy

Expanding freelance market with increasing demand for flexible work arrangements

Untapped Rural Market

Limited access to services in rural areas; community-centric approach builds trust

Technology Adoption

High smartphone penetration and growing e-commerce trends in the Philippines

Partnership Potential

Collaborations with local governments and businesses to expand reach

Current Status

SRV is currently under development by SRV Digital Solutions Co. The project is preparing to launch initially with core features focused on the Baguio market, facilitating cash and GCash transactions while building the user base and refining blockchain-integrated functionalities on test networks.
