SRV: Serbisyo, Rito, Valid

Your Local Service Connection in Baguio

SRV (Serbisyo, Rito, Valid) is a blockchain-powered marketplace connecting clients with local freelance service providers in Baguio City. Developed by SRV Digital Solutions Co., the platform features service discovery, provider profiles, booking systems, and a unique vouching mechanism where users stake coins to build provider trust.

Blockchain Trust: Staking-based vouching system with smart contracts
Categories: Home repairs, auto services, tech support, cleaning, beauty, delivery, tutoring
Payments: Cash & GCash with blockchain integration on test networks
Status: Currently in development, preparing for Baguio market launch
